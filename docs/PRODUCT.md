# Product overview

## Product

**Masar — مسار** is an Egyptian Arabic-first web platform that helps users understand how to complete government procedures.

For example, a user may search for `استخراج بطاقة بدل فاقد` and receive guidance about the procedure.

## Information shown for a procedure

Masar should explain, when the information is officially published:

- Required documents
- Responsible authority
- Step-by-step procedure
- Fees
- Expected duration
- Whether the service is available online
- Official source
- Last verified date

## Product principles

- Masar is a guidance and navigation layer, not a replacement for government services.
- The product must not invent government requirements, fees, durations, or procedures.
- Every factual service detail should eventually have an official source and verification date.
- The interface should feel like a modern, trustworthy Egyptian technology product, not a government website.
- Arabic is the primary language.
- The product must be mobile-first and accessible.
