# Jobs to be done

## Primary job

When I need to complete a government procedure, I want to find trusted, plain-Arabic guidance for the correct service so that I can prepare and take the next official step without a wasted visit or random searching.

## MVP jobs

### Find the correct procedure from my own words

When I describe my need in everyday Arabic, I want to find the relevant procedure even if I do not know its official name.

### Decide whether a result is the right one

When I see a service result, I want to quickly understand what it is for and who handles it so that I do not follow the wrong process.

### Prepare what I need

When I open a procedure, I want to see required documents and steps in a clear order so that I can prepare before acting.

### Know what is confirmed and where it came from

When I rely on a factual detail, I want to see an official source and when it was last verified so that I can judge whether to trust it.

### Continue with the official service when available

When an official online service exists, I want to reach it from the guidance so that I can continue in the right place.

### Recover when I am on the wrong path

When a service is not relevant, I want to return to search or its category without losing my context so that I can find an alternative quickly.

## Not MVP jobs

- Completing a government transaction inside Masar
- Tracking applications or receiving status updates
- Getting personalized legal or administrative advice
- Conversing with an AI assistant about procedures
