# Value proposition

## Core problem

People completing Egyptian government procedures often have to search across unclear or mixed-quality sources. They may not know the official procedure name, the responsible authority, what to prepare, or whether the information is current.

## Masar's solution

Masar organizes verified guidance for selected government procedures into a clear Arabic-first experience. It helps users find a procedure, understand what is officially published about it, and continue to the official source when needed.

Masar does not perform the government transaction or replace the responsible authority.

## Main value proposition

**Find the right government procedure, prepare with confidence, and continue from an official source.**

## Why use Masar instead of searching randomly

- It starts from the user's everyday-language need, not only official terminology.
- It brings the essential preparation information into one scannable service page.
- It clearly identifies the responsible authority.
- It distinguishes factual guidance through an official source and a last verified date.
- It provides a focused path to the official service instead of a broad, mixed set of search results.

## Product trust principles

- Do not invent, estimate, or imply unpublished requirements, fees, durations, or procedures.
- Show factual service details only when backed by an official source.
- Show the official source and last verified date with the service guidance.
- Omit a fee, duration, or online-service claim when it is not officially published; do not fill gaps with guesses.
- Use clear language that separates Masar's guidance from the official service.
- Make uncertainty visible rather than sounding more certain than the source supports.

## Open decision

- The exact source eligibility policy, review workflow, and verification cadence need to be defined before production content is published.
